What it is

Small by design.

One local core, one shell, no server. Every piece is small enough to read — and every view is built from the same append-only log.

01

Less is more

A minimal prompt, a simple interface, fewer tools. Nothing accumulates by accident.

02

Everything is a log

Sessions are append-only. Transcript, trajectory, diff and cost are views over the same log.

03

Permission is a ceiling

A sandbox confines what the agent writes. A wider mode needs a human. rm runs only after the trash has failed.

04

Memory, per chat

Facts, issues and decisions stay with the chat. Keep talking and it grows into scenes and a persona.

05

Extensions, in or out of process

Widgets, commands, themes, services. A packaged extension asks the host through capabilities, never handles.

06

Local, by construction

No account, no server, no telemetry. Keys and data stay on disk.
